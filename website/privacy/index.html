Privacy Policy

Local-First Data & Privacy Policy

ProtoFlow AI (formerly Alex) is architected so your business prototypes, database schemas, and AI credentials stay under your control on your own computer.

1. Local-First Desktop Projects (.pflow)

ProtoFlow AI Desktop (packaged under the Alex application binary) operates without mandatory registration or cloud login. Prototype projects, ER database schemas, sticky-note requirements, project notes, and imported Axure .rp files are parsed and stored locally on your machine as .pflow archives or unpacked directory trees.

2. AI Provider Credentials & Telemetry

  • Your Own Keys or Local Ollama: Model accounts and API keys configured in the AI Management Center are stored locally via OS secure storage and Hermes local runtime profiles — never routed through a proprietary ProtoFlow billing proxy.
  • Opt-In Runtime Diagnostics: Diagnostic capture defaults to local disk and is uploaded only if you explicitly enable sharing or attach a bundle for troubleshooting.
  • Explicit Publishing Only: Pages and schema snapshots are uploaded solely when you explicitly click Publish to send a snapshot to your chosen self-hosted server.

3. Official Website & Browser Demo

This static website (including the static /demo/ sandbox) does not load third-party ad networks or cross-site behavioral trackers. It only stores your chosen language preference (protoflow-locale) in browser localStorage and fetches public release metadata (using credentials: 'omit') from GitHub Releases or your configured update endpoint so you can download verified installers with SHA-256 digests.
